# Comfort Watch

A guide to Korean, Japanese and Chinese dramas, for when you've got dinner in front of you and no idea what to put on.

Pick a region. Pick a few moods. Get a list. That's the whole thing.

**[Open it here](comfort-watch.html)**

## What you'll find

54 shows, 18 each from Korea, Japan and China. All picked by hand, nothing generated or scraped.

Twelve moods to choose from: Romance, Comedy, Thriller/Mystery, Historical, Fantasy/Wuxia, Slice of Life, Family, Workplace, Crime, Coming of Age, Horror/Survival, Melodrama/Revenge. Pick as many as you want and shows that hit more of them float to the top.

Can't even decide on moods? There's a Surprise Me button. And if you leave everything blank you'll get six featured picks to start from.

Every show has a short description of what it's actually about, plus a link to its Wikipedia page if you want the cast, the episode count, or a look at the poster.

## Reading it in your language

There's a switcher in the top right corner: English, 한국어, 日本語, 中文.

It changes everything, not just the buttons. All 54 descriptions are written out in each language. Show titles stay in English though, since that's what you'd type into Netflix or Viki when you go looking for one.

## A couple of things to know

**It won't tell you where to stream something.** That changes constantly and differs by country, so anything listed here would be wrong within a month. Find something you like, then search it wherever you normally watch.

**The posters live on Wikipedia, not here.** Most drama posters are licensed so that only Wikipedia can display them, so each show links out to its article instead.

## Where the picks come from

These are shows worth someone's evening, not whatever's trending. Some are famous, some less so. The mix leans toward things that hold up: Reply 1988, Nirvana in Fire, My Mister, Midnight Diner, The Untamed, Hospital Playlist.

Horror is a bit thin, especially on the Japanese and Chinese side. That's a real gap in what's out there rather than an oversight.
